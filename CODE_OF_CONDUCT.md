# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the CoT-Guard community welcoming and free
from harassment for everyone, regardless of identity, background, appearance,
experience, education, nationality, disability, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes being respectful, showing empathy, accepting
constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and acting in the
community's best interest.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention,
trolling, personal attacks, publishing private information without permission,
and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and Enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone officially
represents the project. Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject content
and contributions that violate these standards. Depending on the severity or
frequency of a violation, maintainers may issue a correction or warning, impose
a temporary ban, or impose a permanent ban.

Report violations to [ndiwan2@illinois.edu](mailto:ndiwan2@illinois.edu).
Reports will be reviewed fairly, and the privacy and safety of reporters will
be respected.
